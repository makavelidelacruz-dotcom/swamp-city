Swamp City asset pack
All 3D models in this folder are by Kenney (https://kenney.nl) and are CC0 1.0 licensed
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required; credit is appreciated.

Source repositories (textures embedded into each GLB with gltf-transform):
- KenneyNL/starter-kit-racing        @ 2f2e5f2646dda89cb21d4e8539bab60c6e955dc8  (trucks, motorcycle, tents)
- KenneyNL/starter-kit-city-builder  @ 4535092b740b378b700efd9df9e27a631815b84a  (buildings, garage, fountain)
- KenneyNL/starter-kit-3d-platformer @ 3fa8a04b1c01ab23db43123d4ce814a34c3fc7f0  (cloud, coin, flag)

Nature models (deadtree-*, normaltree-*, bush*, plant-flowers, grass-large, rock-*):
from "Ultimate Stylized Nature" by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0, taken from the
AllModels.blend file supplied by the world owner. Converted to GLB in Blender 5.3, textures reduced
to 512 px. Textures that were not packed in the .blend were replaced: dead trees and rocks use flat
colours, and the leafy trees reuse the pack's bush-leaf texture.

Medieval models (med-house, med-townhouse, med-hall, med-cottage, med-wagon, med-crate, med-fence, med-ironfence):
from the "Medieval Village MegaKit [Standard]" by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0, supplied by the
world owner. The four houses were assembled from the kit's modular pieces in Blender 5.3; normal and roughness
maps were removed and textures reduced to 512 px WebP to keep downloads small.

Downtown models (dt-large, dt-medium, dt-small, dt-acunit, dt-bollard, dt-planter, dt-manhole):
from the "Downtown City MegaKit [Standard]" by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0, supplied by the
world owner. Ready-made buildings were simplified in Blender 5.3 (coplanar faces merged, 25-42% fewer
triangles), the kit's wear-mask vertex colours removed, textures reduced to 512 px WebP and geometry quantized.
